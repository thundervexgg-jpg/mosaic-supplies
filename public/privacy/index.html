Privacy notice

Mosaic Supplies Ltd ("we") is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: am@mosaic-supplies.co.uk.

What we hold

  • Business contacts: names, job titles, email addresses and phone numbers of people at suppliers, customers and partners who contact us or whom we contact about trade.
  • Order data: records of purchases and sales needed to run the business and meet accounting and tax obligations.
  • Bank data: read-only account details, balances and transactions for the company's own bank accounts, accessed through Enable Banking with the account holder's consent. This is not data about website visitors.

Why, and on what basis

To run our trading relationships and keep accurate business records (legitimate interests and contract), and to meet legal obligations such as bookkeeping and tax (legal obligation). Bank access is used only for the company's own financial management.

Where it is kept

Bank data is accessed on a read-only basis and held securely on company-controlled systems within the UK, with access restricted to authorised directors. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for marketing. Other business records are held in our secure email and business systems.

How long

Business and order records are kept for up to six years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK tax law requires. Bank access consent lasts at most 180 days and can be withdrawn at any time.

This website

This site uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If you use the enquiry form, it opens your own email program; nothing is stored by the site.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to its use, by emailing am@mosaic-supplies.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
